ACCOUNTABILITY

Who is responsible for VISIONE Wire.

VISIONE Wire is an independent editorial project operated by VISIONE and edited through the VISIONE Wire desk. Public responsibility for corrections, editorial inquiries and site operations is handled through the newsroom contact listed on this site.

The site currently publishes under the organization byline VISIONE Wire. That byline means the article was prepared, checked and published as part of the publication's editorial workflow rather than as an anonymous scrape from another site. When a story requires a special disclosure, correction or update note, that note should be added directly to the article.

Correction requests should include the article URL, the disputed sentence, the reason it is inaccurate and a reliable source or document that supports the correction.

What we verify before publishing

For news articles, the desk looks for primary sources first: official announcements, regulatory filings, court documents, company newsrooms, public agencies, earnings releases, research reports, event pages, store listings and official trailers or videos. Secondary sources are used when they add confirmation, reporting, market reaction or context that a primary document does not provide.

We label uncertainty. If a number comes from a company, court filing, regulator or media report, the article should say so. If VISIONE Wire is making an inference, the article should not present that inference as a fact.

Corrections and updates

Small edits for clarity, spelling, formatting or link maintenance may be made without a formal correction note. Material factual corrections should be disclosed in the article when they change the meaning of the report or could have affected a reader's understanding.

When an ongoing story changes materially, VISIONE Wire may update the existing URL instead of creating a new article. In those cases, the article should show a modified date and the homepage may label the card as updated.

Image and video transparency

Images should come from official press material, public-domain sources, licensed repositories, Wikimedia Commons with a compatible license, government media libraries or previously hosted VISIONE assets. Image credits and license notes are included when required or editorially useful.

VISIONE Wire does not download and re-upload third-party videos as if they were its own. When official video materially improves a story, the article embeds the official player or links to the original source. If no reliable video exists, the article should say that no video was used instead of padding the page with unrelated media.

AI assistance

AI tools may help with research organization, drafting, summarization support, translation and editing. They are not treated as a source, and factual claims must be checked against external evidence before publication. AI assistance is not a license to publish thin rewrites, invented details or articles created only to capture search traffic.

Commercial independence

Advertising supports the publication, but advertisers do not buy editorial outcomes. Paid placements, affiliate relationships or sponsorships must be labeled. Independent editorial coverage should remain separate from commercial arrangements.

Corrections log

As of August 30, 2026, VISIONE Wire does not maintain a separate public corrections archive because significant corrections are disclosed on the affected article. If correction volume grows, this page may be expanded into a dated corrections log.
