Advertising & Sponsorship Standards

Advertising helps fund VISIONE Wire, but commercial relationships do not buy editorial conclusions.

Clear labeling

Paid placements, sponsored articles, affiliate relationships and other commercial material must be clearly distinguishable from independent editorial reporting.

Editorial independence

Advertisers do not receive the right to approve or rewrite independent coverage about them. Commercial relationships that create a material conflict for a story should be disclosed.

Advertising density

Ads should not overwhelm the article, obstruct navigation or make it difficult to distinguish reporting from commercial content. VISIONE Wire reserves space for advertising but prioritizes the reading experience.

Affiliate links

When an article contains affiliate links that may generate a commission, the relationship will be disclosed. Editorial recommendations should not be based solely on commission value.
