EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Fast, clear and accountable.

VISIONE Wire covers global news, business, markets, technology, artificial intelligence, gaming, entertainment, policy and culture. Our aim is to publish quickly without lowering our standards for sourcing, attribution, clarity or corrections.

Originality

We do not publish copied articles or mass-generated rewrites. Every report should add original structure, synthesis, context, verification or analysis beyond the source material.

Sources and attribution

Claims that depend on external reporting, company announcements, research, public records or other outside information should be linked or clearly attributed. Primary sources are preferred whenever practical.

Use of AI

AI tools may assist with research support, transcription, translation, drafting and editing. They are not treated as a source. Material factual claims must be checked against reliable information before publication, and AI-assisted output is reviewed before publishing.

Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected promptly. When a correction changes the meaning of a report, the article should disclose that correction and, when useful, the time or date it was made.

Headlines

Headlines should accurately reflect the article. We avoid misleading certainty, deceptive clickbait and wording that materially overstates what the reporting supports.

Commercial separation

Sponsored or paid material must be clearly labeled and kept distinct from independent editorial work. See our Advertising & Sponsorship Standards.

Scope

VISIONE Wire is a broad digital publication rather than a narrow vertical. Coverage can move across news, markets, technology, games, entertainment, policy, business and culture whenever the story justifies it.
