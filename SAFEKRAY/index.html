Community intelligence, in motion

From a local observation to shared awareness.

If someone hears a drone explosion, sees smoke or finds a blocked road, they can report it at once. Nearby people then help the information become more useful — or stop it from becoming misleading.

01

A person reports

A structured category, approximate location and time create a civilian report without exposing sensitive military information.

02

Others respond

People in the area confirm or challenge what they also heard or observed, adding fast local context.

03

Confidence changes

Confirmations and contradictory votes change the current community status. Proximity and timing are planned checks for stronger location confidence.

Transparent community validation

Trust is a signal, not a label.

Reports currently become community confirmed after at least three confirmations and more confirmations than rejections. Community information remains visually distinct from official alerts, so users can understand the source and act with appropriate caution.

Location-integrity safeguards

Built to make location abuse harder.

Sensitive civilian information needs more than a pin on a map. The current Android app checks the device's reported country. Stronger anti-abuse measures are being designed and are not yet a guarantee.

VPN

Anti-VPN barriers

Planned: evaluate VPN and proxy signals to reduce disguised access and coordinated abuse. This is not yet implemented in the current app.

RU

Russian geolocation blocking

Current Android access gate blocks when the device reports a location in Russia. This device-side check can be bypassed and is not server enforcement.

◎

Location confidence checks

Planned: assess suspicious location inconsistencies before nearby actions count as credible. The current report score does not yet use location confidence.

i
A support layer — never a replacement for authorities.SAFEKRAY does not present community reports as official emergency information. During danger, users should follow official alerts, emergency services and local authorities.
