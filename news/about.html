ABOUT

A small desk built for fast-moving stories.

VISIONE Wire is an experimental independent publication from VISIONE focused on artificial intelligence, technology and digital business.

The project is testing a simple idea: modern publishing infrastructure can make a small editorial operation fast without turning it into an article-copying factory. We prioritize primary sources, clear attribution, useful context and visible corrections.

Coverage

Our initial beats are artificial intelligence, consumer and enterprise technology, software platforms, startups, SaaS, digital commerce and internet-native business models.

Ownership

VISIONE Wire is operated as a pilot publication by VISIONE. It is not affiliated with Google News, Google, or the companies it may cover.

Editorial policy
