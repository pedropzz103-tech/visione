ACCOUNTABILITY

A publication should show who is responsible for it.

VISIONE Wire is an independent editorial project operated by VISIONE. Pedro is the editor responsible for story selection, source review, corrections and the publication workflow.

Correction requests should include the article URL, the disputed statement, why it is inaccurate and a reliable source or document supporting the correction.

What is checked before publication

The desk looks for primary sources first when practical: official announcements, regulatory filings, court documents, company newsrooms, technical documentation, public agencies, research reports and datasets. Independent reporting is used to confirm events, add context and challenge claims made by interested parties.

Articles should identify the origin of important claims. A company estimate is not presented as an independently measured fact. A lawsuit allegation is not treated as a court finding. A VISIONE Wire inference is labeled as analysis rather than hidden inside factual prose.

Originality and reuse

The publication does not exist to mass-produce rewrites. New articles must add original value through source synthesis, chronology, comparison, calculation, document analysis, contextual benchmarking or another meaningful contribution. If that contribution is absent, the article should not be published as a new URL.

Corrections and updates

Material factual errors are corrected promptly. If a correction changes the meaning of a report or could have affected a reader's understanding, the article should disclose the correction. Ongoing stories may be updated on the same URL, with a visible modified date or update note when the change is substantial.

Image and video transparency

Images should come from official press material, public-domain sources, compatible licensed repositories, government libraries or VISIONE-owned assets. Rights and credit information are included where required or editorially useful. Third-party video is embedded or linked from its official source rather than downloaded and re-uploaded.

AI assistance

AI tools may help organize research, draft, translate and edit. AI is not a factual source and does not replace source verification or editorial judgment. The publication's standards prohibit invented quotes, sources, names, credentials, dates, specifications and legal conclusions.

Commercial independence

Advertising can support the publication, but advertisers do not control independent reporting. Sponsored or paid material must be labeled. Commercial relationships do not grant a right to approve headlines, remove unfavorable facts or rewrite editorial conclusions.
