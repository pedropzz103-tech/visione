Advertising & Sponsorship Standards

Advertising can help fund VISIONE Wire, but commercial relationships do not buy editorial conclusions, article placement, headlines or source interpretation. Readers should be able to tell the difference between independent reporting and paid material without needing to guess.

Clear labeling

Paid placements, sponsored articles, affiliate relationships, partner posts and other commercial material must be clearly distinguishable from independent editorial reporting. Labels should appear where readers see the material, not only in a hidden disclosure page.

Editorial independence

Advertisers do not receive the right to approve, remove or rewrite independent coverage about them. Commercial relationships that create a material conflict for a story should be disclosed in or near the article.

Advertising density and user experience

Ads should not overwhelm the article, obstruct navigation, imitate editorial links, cover content, push the main text out of reach or make it difficult to distinguish reporting from commercial content. VISIONE Wire may reserve space for advertising, but the reading experience comes first.

Affiliate links

When an article contains affiliate links that may generate a commission, the relationship will be disclosed. Editorial recommendations should not be based solely on commission value, and an affiliate link should never change the factual description of a product, game, service or company.

Restricted and unsuitable material

VISIONE Wire does not seek advertising that is illegal, deceptive, malicious, sexually explicit, hateful, designed to impersonate editorial content or otherwise incompatible with a general news and technology publication. Advertising partners remain responsible for complying with applicable laws and platform policies.
