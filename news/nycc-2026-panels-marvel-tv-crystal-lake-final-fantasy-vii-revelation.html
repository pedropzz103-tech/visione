NYCC 2026 panel slate brings Marvel TV, Crystal Lake, Final Fantasy VII Revelation and more

New York Comic Con’s 20th anniversary lineup is taking shape, with Marvel, horror franchises, anime, games and major TV panels already on the schedule.

By VISIONE Wire · August 19, 2026 · 19:46 CEST

The first major programming slate for New York Comic Con 2026 has landed, and the October convention is stacking together Marvel Television, horror franchises, anime, games and major streaming series for its 20th anniversary.

Entertainment Weekly published the first confirmed panel details on August 19. NYCC itself confirms the convention will run October 8 through October 11 at the Javits Center in Manhattan.

Marvel, Crystal Lake and Dexter are on the slate

Marvel Television and Marvel Animation will host a panel focused on upcoming Disney+ series. Peacock and A24 are bringing an exclusive first-look screening of Crystal Lake, the prequel to the Friday the 13th franchise, followed by a discussion with creator Brad Caleb Kane and members of the cast.

Dexter: Resurrection will also hold a season-two panel with the cast and creative team, while AMC+ is planning a spotlight that includes Yaga, Mayfair Witches and The Walking Dead: Daryl Dixon.

Final Fantasy and anime fans have reasons to watch

One of the biggest gaming sessions currently listed is Final Fantasy VII Revelation — Meet the English Voice Cast, which EW says will bring the main English voice cast together with director Naoki Hamaguchi and include behind-the-scenes recording footage.

The schedule also includes One Piece programming from Toei Animation and Crunchyroll, a Gundam showcase, manga and manhwa panels, and additional anime programming.

Why this matters months before October

Convention schedules create repeated news cycles. A panel announcement generates one round of interest, then casting confirmations, trailers, first-look footage and surprise announcements create another. That makes NYCC less of a single story and more of a pipeline of entertainment stories through October.

The current lineup is not final. NYCC says more guests, panels and surprises will be announced as the event approaches.
