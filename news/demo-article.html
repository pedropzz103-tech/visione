VISIONE Wire completes its publishing infrastructure prototype

A deliberately non-indexed sample shows how future sourced articles will be structured, marked up and published.

By VISIONE Wire · August 19, 2026 · Prototype

Prototype: This page is intentionally excluded from search indexing and Google News. It is not presented as a real news event.

VISIONE Wire has completed the first version of its static publishing template. The prototype includes article metadata, structured data, canonical URLs, responsive typography and editorial transparency pages.

Why the first article is not fake news

A news site needs real reporting before it needs volume. Instead of padding the launch with fabricated stories or rewritten articles, this prototype is marked noindex and excluded from the news sitemap. The first indexable report will be based on a real, current event and reliable sources.

What the publishing pipeline supports

Future reports can include publication and modification dates, authorship, source links, corrections, social metadata and NewsArticle structured data. New stories published within the relevant window can also be added to the dedicated Google News sitemap.

What comes next

The next stage is editorial rather than cosmetic: select a current story, research primary sources, publish an original article, add it to the feeds and submit the site’s sitemaps through Google Search Console.
