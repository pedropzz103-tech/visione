PlayStation Plus September 2026 games: Sniper Elite, MLB The Show 26, Wobbly Life and Chained Echoes

Sony has confirmed an unusually broad four-game Monthly Games lineup for September, with tactical sniping, baseball, co-op sandbox play and a retro-styled JRPG all arriving on the same day.

By VISIONE Wire · August 26, 2026 · 20:01 CEST

Sony Interactive Entertainment has announced four PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for September 2026: Sniper Elite: Resistance, MLB The Show 26, Wobbly Life and Chained Echoes. All four become available to PlayStation Plus members on Tuesday, September 1, and remain claimable through Monday, October 5.

The lineup is notable for both its size and range. Sony usually builds the Monthly Games offer around a compact monthly selection, but September spreads across a World War II tactical shooter, a current sports release, a physics-heavy co-op sandbox and a critically regarded role-playing game styled after the 16-bit era. Platform support also spans PS5 and PS4 rather than pushing the entire bundle toward current-generation hardware.

September 2026 PlayStation Plus games at a glance

Sniper Elite: Resistance is available on PS5 and PS4. Rebellion's tactical third-person shooter runs parallel to the events of Sniper Elite 5 and centers on occupied France. The campaign supports co-op, while Axis Invasion returns as a competitive mode in which another player can enter the campaign as an enemy sniper. Sony also highlights 16-player competitive multiplayer and a Survival mode for teams of up to four players.

MLB The Show 26 is available on PS5. Sony describes this year's baseball release as an expanded version of its long-running simulation, with Road to the Show additions, new college teams and further Diamond Dynasty content. PlayStation Plus members also receive access to a limited-time Jump Start Bundle containing five The Show Packs, an Equipment Pack and a Jumpstart Choice Pack. Sony says that extra bundle will be available from September 1 through October 6.

Wobbly Life comes to PS5 and PS4. The open-world sandbox supports solo play and up to four players in online or local split-screen co-op. Its appeal is less about a traditional campaign and more about interactive environments, jobs, mini-games, exploration and intentionally chaotic physics. Arcade modes such as Trash Zone, Hide & Seek, Wobble Run and Sandbox extend the party-game side of the package.

Chained Echoes is the PS4 title in the lineup and remains playable on PS5 through backward compatibility. The single-player RPG follows a group of heroes across Valandis during a war involving three kingdoms. Its combat and presentation deliberately evoke the 16-bit role-playing era, while its setting mixes fantasy staples such as dragons and magic with piloted mechanical suits.

The exact dates that matter

The September Monthly Games go live on September 1, 2026. Members have until October 5 to add them to their libraries. As with previous PlayStation Plus Monthly Games, access remains tied to an active PlayStation Plus subscription after a title has been claimed.

There is also a deadline for the outgoing August lineup. Sony says members have until August 31 to add Dying Light 2 Stay Human: Reloaded Edition, Big Walk and Signalis to their libraries. Anyone interested in those titles should claim them before the September rotation begins.

Sony notes that Monthly Games availability can differ by region. That caveat is important for readers outside the largest PlayStation Store markets, because individual games, editions or entitlements can occasionally vary depending on local publishing arrangements.

Why September's lineup stands out

The strongest strategic choice here is variety. Sniper Elite: Resistance gives Sony a recognizable action game with campaign co-op and multiplayer longevity. MLB The Show 26 adds a relatively recent first-party sports title in a month when the baseball season is approaching its decisive stretch. Wobbly Life supplies family-friendly and social play, while Chained Echoes targets players looking for a focused single-player RPG rather than a service game.

The four games also occupy different time commitments. A player can dip into a round of Wobbly Life with friends, spend dozens of hours in Diamond Dynasty, play through the Sniper Elite campaign cooperatively or treat Chained Echoes as a complete narrative RPG. That breadth increases the chance that at least one part of the lineup has value for different kinds of subscribers.

There is a business angle too. Subscription services are under pressure to make recurring fees feel tangible every month. Sony's decision to headline four games and include a current annual sports release gives September a clearer marketing hook than a lineup dominated by older catalog titles.

What is confirmed and what is not

Confirmed: all four games join the Monthly Games lineup on September 1; the claim window runs through October 5; Sniper Elite: Resistance and Wobbly Life support PS5 and PS4; MLB The Show 26 is listed for PS5; and Chained Echoes is listed for PS4.

Confirmed separately: PlayStation Plus members receive the limited-time MLB The Show 26 Jump Start Bundle from September 1 through October 6.

Regional uncertainty: Sony explicitly warns that the Monthly Games lineup may differ by region. The safest way to confirm a local entitlement is to check the PlayStation Store when the September lineup goes live.

No ownership without membership: claiming a Monthly Game does not turn it into a permanently playable purchase independent of PlayStation Plus. Continued access requires an active qualifying subscription.

What happens next

The immediate next step is simple: August subscribers have until August 31 to claim the outgoing games, and the September package arrives the following day. After that, the most useful comparison will be how the four Monthly Games sit alongside September's wider PlayStation Plus Game Catalog additions, which Sony announces separately.

For now, September's Monthly Games are unusually easy to summarize: four distinct genres, two console generations and one clear availability window. The lineup does not depend on a single blockbuster to carry the month, which may be its biggest strength.

Bottom line

PlayStation Plus members get Sniper Elite: Resistance, MLB The Show 26, Wobbly Life and Chained Echoes from September 1 through October 5. Sony has paired one of its own current sports games with tactical action, co-op chaos and a respected indie RPG, creating a broader-than-usual Monthly Games package as the service enters the autumn release season.
