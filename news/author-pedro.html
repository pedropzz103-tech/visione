EDITOR

Pedro

Editor, VISIONE Wire

Pedro is the editor responsible for VISIONE Wire. His role covers story selection, source review, publication decisions, corrections and the editorial workflow used to turn verified source material into reader-focused reporting and analysis.

Editorial responsibilities

The editor decides whether a story is important enough to publish, checks the source trail behind material claims, separates facts from interested-party statements and reviews whether an article adds original value beyond summarizing its sources.

VISIONE Wire deliberately publishes less when the available evidence is weak. The publication's normal beat is artificial intelligence, software and developer infrastructure, chips and compute, cybersecurity, digital business and technology policy.

Use of AI

AI tools may assist with research organization, translation, drafting and editing inside the workflow. They are not treated as sources and do not replace verification against primary documents, credible reporting or other external evidence.

Standards and corrections

The standards used for sourcing, originality, images, AI assistance and commercial separation are published in the Editorial Standards. The Accountability & Transparency page explains responsibility and correction handling in more detail.
