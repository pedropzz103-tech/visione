ADVERTISING

Advertising should never masquerade as reporting.

VISIONE Wire is configured to use Google AdSense as a potential source of publication revenue. Ad code being present does not mean ads will always be served; serving depends on account status, site approval, user region, consent requirements, inventory and Google's systems.

Editorial independence

Advertisers do not receive the right to approve, remove or rewrite independent coverage about them. Advertising relationships do not determine story selection, factual conclusions, headlines or source interpretation.

Clear labeling

Sponsored articles, paid placements, affiliate relationships, partner posts or other commercial material must be clearly distinguishable from independent editorial reporting where they appear. A disclosure hidden only on this page is not sufficient for paid content.

Ad density and reader experience

Editorial content should remain the dominant element of every article. Ads should not cover text, imitate editorial links, block navigation or create a page that contains more commercial surface than useful reporting. Redirect pages and navigation-only utility pages should not carry advertising code.

Affiliate links

If an article uses affiliate links that may generate commission, that relationship must be disclosed near the relevant content. Commission value should not determine the factual description or editorial recommendation.

Commercial inquiries

Advertising and sponsorship questions can be sent to pedropzz103@gmail.com. Commercial inquiries are handled separately from corrections, source material and editorial feedback.
