Gamescom 2026 Opening Night Live: Witcher 3 expansion, dates and what is confirmed

The Cologne show is entering its biggest edition yet, with more than 1,600 exhibitors, a record 233,000 square metres of exhibition space and a first public look at The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past.

By VISIONE Wire · Published August 19, 2026 · Updated August 23, 2026 · 12:00 CEST

Gamescom 2026 is about to turn Cologne into the center of the games industry. The main consumer show runs from August 26 through August 30, while Opening Night Live takes place on Tuesday, August 25. Organizers describe the 2026 edition as the largest in the event’s history, with more than 1,600 exhibitors from 67 countries occupying 233,000 square metres of gross exhibition space.

That scale matters because gamescom is no longer simply a European trade fair. It is simultaneously a physical expo, a global marketing stage and a week-long stream of announcements. The most important early search moment will be Opening Night Live, hosted by Geoff Keighley, where publishers are expected to debut trailers, release information and world premieres before the show floor opens.

What is confirmed for gamescom 2026

The event itself begins with gamescom dev, the developer conference at Koelnmesse, followed by Opening Night Live on August 25 and the main gamescom halls from August 26 to August 30. Gamescom says all available exhibition space is booked for the first time. Its August 18 figures list more than 1,600 exhibitors from 67 countries, 47 country pavilions representing 40 countries and an expanded indie area with hundreds of companies.

Those are confirmed organizer figures, not attendance forecasts. The final visitor total will only be known after the event. What can already be said is that the commercial footprint is larger than last year and that the show is drawing companies from across the console, PC, mobile, publishing, hardware and independent-development markets.

The official exhibitor material currently highlights companies and games including Nintendo, Xbox, Ubisoft, Capcom, Bandai Namco, Electronic Arts, Sega, NetEase, HoYoverse and CD Projekt Red. The exact composition of Opening Night Live can still change until broadcast, so VISIONE Wire is treating only officially announced appearances as confirmed.

The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past is now a full-fledged expansion

The biggest material update since this article was first published concerns The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past. CD Projekt Red and gamescom now describe it explicitly as a full-fledged expansion, not a minor content update.

The expansion is being created in partnership with Fool’s Theory, a studio whose team includes veteran developers who previously worked on The Witcher 3. Players will return as Geralt of Rivia in a new adventure positioned before the franchise moves forward with Ciri in the upcoming The Witcher 4.

CD Projekt says Songs of the Past is coming in 2027 for PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The company has also confirmed that viewers will get a glimpse during Opening Night Live on August 25. Starting August 26, attendees in Cologne will be able to see the first showcase of the expansion in the entertainment area. Business-area presentations are scheduled from August 26 through August 28, while entertainment-area showcases run through August 30.

The timing gives CD Projekt a powerful bridge between two eras of the series. The Witcher 3 launched in 2015 and has sold more than 65 million copies according to the company. A substantial expansion more than a decade later can reactivate that enormous installed audience while also preparing players for The Witcher 4.

Why the Witcher announcement is unusually important

There is a meaningful distinction between a remaster-style update, a small quest pack and a full expansion. CD Projekt built its reputation partly on large post-launch expansions such as Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine. Calling Songs of the Past “full-fledged” therefore creates expectations about scope, story and production value that a simple downloadable add-on would not carry.

What is not yet confirmed is equally important. CD Projekt has not publicly given an exact 2027 release date, final price, total playtime or complete story synopsis. Those details should not be inferred from the studio’s older expansions. Gamescom is expected to clarify at least some of them, but until the presentations happen they remain open questions.

Gamescom is also becoming a bigger business event

The public show is only one layer of gamescom week. Organizers are also expanding the business program around developers, investment and policy. Gamescom dev runs around the opening of the week, the business area operates from August 26 to August 28, gamescom congress takes place on August 27 and the gamescom invest circle is also scheduled for August 27.

Gamescom says the broader business program connects more than 30,000 professionals. The event’s political footprint is growing as well: Germany’s Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier is scheduled to become the first German head of state to attend gamescom, while the congress program includes discussions on games, democracy, regulation and the wider economy.

What viewers should watch on August 25

Opening Night Live remains the highest-impact moment for people outside Cologne. The format concentrates trailers and announcements into a global livestream, which typically creates immediate demand for release dates, gameplay footage, platforms and pre-order information.

For VISIONE Wire, the editorial rule is simple: major reveals will be covered as individual stories rather than buried inside a giant roundup. The Witcher 3 expansion is already substantial enough to merit close follow-up. Other games shown during the broadcast will only be treated as confirmed once their publishers or the event itself publish the underlying details.

No official 2026 Opening Night Live video page with stable metadata was available from the event at the time of this update, so this article does not embed an unofficial mirror. Readers can follow the original event channels from the gamescom website, which links its official broadcast destinations.

The bottom line

Gamescom 2026 opens with unusual momentum: record exhibition space, more than 1,600 exhibitors and a major late-life expansion for one of the best-selling RPGs of the modern era. The confirmed facts already justify attention, but the largest wave of news is still ahead. August 25 will determine which announcements become the week’s defining stories, while August 26 begins the hands-on and show-floor phase in Cologne.
