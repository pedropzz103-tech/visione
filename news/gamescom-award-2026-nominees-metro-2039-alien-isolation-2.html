Gamescom Award 2026 winners: Witcher 3 and Sega take three awards each

The final results overturn the nomination-day narrative: Metro 2039 takes Best Visuals, but CD Projekt Red and Sega leave Cologne with the broadest trophy haul across 19 categories.

By VISIONE Wire · Published August 25, 2026 · Updated August 29, 2026 · 08:20 CEST

The Gamescom Award 2026 winners are official, and the final scoreboard looks very different from the nominee list that opened the week. CD Projekt Red's presence around The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt collected three awards, while Sega also finished with three trophies spread across Alien: Isolation 2, Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000 and its overall publisher lineup.

Capcom and Ubisoft won two awards each. Metro 2039, which entered the show as the most-nominated single game with five nominations, won Best Visuals. Games Done Quick received the special HEART OF GAMING Award as the charity speedrunning event made its European gamescom debut.

The official results were announced Friday, August 28, across 19 categories at the gamescom studio in Cologne. The awards combine jury judging with community voting in selected categories, so the final list reflects both professional evaluation and audience preference rather than a single editorial ranking.

The Witcher 3 turns its gamescom comeback into three trophies

CD Projekt Red's strongest showing came not from a platform category, but from the way it presented The Witcher 3 at the event. The remaster and the Songs of the Past expansion won Best Trailer / Announcement, a consumer-voted category covering reveals shown during Opening Night Live.

The same franchise also won Best Merch for the pin and medallion offered around the DLC booth, while the booth itself received the jury's Best Booth award. Together, those wins gave The Witcher 3 three trophies and made CD Projekt Red one of the event's two biggest award winners.

The result is especially notable because the game is not a new franchise launch. The remaster revives a 2015 title, while Songs of the Past extends it with new paid content. Gamescom voters and jurors effectively rewarded the entire return package: announcement, physical show presence and merchandise.

Sega matches CD Projekt Red with three awards

Sega's three trophies were spread across multiple titles and one publisher-level category. Alien: Isolation 2 won Best Audio, giving Creative Assembly recognition in one of the craft categories where it had been heavily nominated.

Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000, also from Creative Assembly and Sega, won Best PC Game. Sega then added Best Lineup, a jury award that evaluates the breadth and strength of an exhibitor's overall portfolio rather than a single title.

That broad result validates the publisher's nomination-day strength. Sega-linked titles entered the week with eight nominations in total, more than any other publisher grouping highlighted by gamescom. The company did not convert all of those nominations into wins, but it did finish tied for the most trophies overall.

Metro 2039 wins Best Visuals after leading nominations

Metro 2039 entered Gamescom Award week with five nominations, the highest total for a single game. Its final win came in Best Visuals, where it beat a field that included Alien: Isolation 2, DEPERSON, Forza Horizon 6 and Inkblood.

That means the most-nominated game did not dominate the final trophy count. It still secured one of the most visible craft awards, but the results underline why nominations should not be treated as forecasts. Gamescom's process uses category-specific judging and, in many cases, community voting after the nominees are announced.

The other Arts winners were Alien: Isolation 2 for Best Audio, Onimusha: Way of the Sword for Best Gameplay, Wanderburg for Most Entertaining, Tides of Annihilation for Most Epic, Finding Polka for Most Wholesome, and SOKO 1977: Anti-Terror Task Force for Games for Impact.

Onimusha and Ubisoft each take two

Capcom's Onimusha: Way of the Sword won both Best Gameplay and Best Sony PlayStation Game. That combination is significant because it pairs a craft award focused on how the game plays with a platform award positioning it as the top PlayStation title in gamescom's submitted field.

Ubisoft also finished with two trophies. Rayman Legends Retold won Best Nintendo Switch 2 Game, while Ubisoft's Business Lounge received the jury award for Best Business Booth.

Elsewhere in the Platform group, Forza Horizon 6 won Best Microsoft Xbox Game, Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000 took Best PC Game, and Where Winds Meet won Best Mobile Game.

The complete platform results

The platform awards provide one of the clearest snapshots of gamescom's 2026 competitive field. Forza Horizon 6 finished ahead of Gears of War: E-Day, Halo: Campaign Evolved, Metro 2039 and STRANGER THAN HEAVEN in the Xbox category.

On PC, Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000 beat Alien: Isolation 2, Crimson Desert, Guns of Eschaton and Metro 2039. On PlayStation, Onimusha: Way of the Sword won against Dragon's Dogma 2 Dark Arisen, Metro 2039, The Blood of Dawnwalker and Tides of Annihilation.

The Switch 2 award went to Rayman Legends Retold, and the mobile trophy went to Where Winds Meet. Those are gamescom-specific awards, meaning the winner is the strongest entry among eligible and submitted games at the event, not a universal ranking of every release on the platform.

Games Done Quick receives the HEART OF GAMING Award

The special HEART OF GAMING Award went to Games Done Quick. Gamescom highlighted the speedrunning organization for its charity work, noting that GDQ has raised more than $50 million for charitable causes over its history.

The award also lands during a milestone weekend for the organization. Gamescom GDQ runs from August 28 through August 30, marking the first time the event has taken place in Europe as part of gamescom. Gamescom says donations and sponsorship proceeds from this edition will go to Gaming for Democracy, an initiative focused on democratic values and social engagement through gaming culture.

What changed from the nominee story

This article originally tracked the nomination field, where Metro 2039 led with five nominations and Alien: Isolation 2 and Denshattack! followed with four each. The winners materially change that picture.

Denshattack! did not convert its four nominations into a trophy. Alien: Isolation 2 converted four nominations into Best Audio. Metro 2039 turned five nominations into Best Visuals. Meanwhile, The Witcher 3 emerged as the strongest overall single-franchise presence through three Global-category wins.

That distinction matters for search and context. A nomination count measures breadth of recognition before voting closes. The final awards measure category outcomes after jury and audience input. They are related signals, but they answer different questions.

Why these awards matter, and why they have limits

Gamescom is one of the world's largest gaming events, and its awards arrive at a moment when publishers are fighting for global attention across trailers, playable demos, press coverage and social conversation. A trophy can strengthen the marketing narrative around a game heading into release.

But the awards are not a substitute for reviews or long-term player reception. Eligibility depends on participation and submission, and many games are judged before their final retail versions exist. Platform awards in particular should be read as awards within the gamescom field, not declarations that one game is objectively the best release of the year.

The strongest takeaway from 2026 is therefore about momentum. CD Projekt Red successfully turned an older blockbuster back into a current-event story. Sega demonstrated portfolio breadth. Metro 2039 maintained its visual prestige even without sweeping its five categories. Capcom's Onimusha emerged with both gameplay and PlayStation recognition.

What happens next

Gamescom continues through Sunday, August 30. The awards are finished, but show-floor demos, developer interviews, Games Done Quick and the final weekend coverage can still change which titles leave Cologne with the most audience momentum.

For the award record itself, the official result is settled: CD Projekt Red and Sega lead with three trophies each, Capcom and Ubisoft take two each, Metro 2039 wins Best Visuals, and Games Done Quick receives the HEART OF GAMING Award.
