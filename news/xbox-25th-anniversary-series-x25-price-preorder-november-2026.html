Xbox 25th Anniversary console costs $899.99: preorder time, launch date and what is included

Microsoft has put a price and a preorder schedule on its translucent-green Xbox Series X25 Limited Edition, turning the anniversary hardware from a showcase reveal into a real November product.

By VISIONE Wire · August 26, 2026 · 18:00 CEST

Microsoft has priced the Xbox 25th Anniversary Collection at $899.99 in the United States and confirmed that the Xbox Series X25 Limited Edition launches on November 13, 2026. The bundle combines a 1 TB Series X-class console in translucent OG Green with the matching X25 Special Edition Wireless Controller and a full-game download of Halo: Campaign Evolved.

The company published the final retail details on August 26, after first revealing the anniversary hardware during the Xbox Games Showcase in June. The headline is not just the color. Every console is individually numbered with a unique engraving, positioning the machine as a collector edition rather than a routine seasonal shell.

Microsoft says selected dedicated Xbox fans are receiving invitation emails on August 26. Broad public preorders in select markets begin Thursday, August 27 at 7 a.m. Pacific, which is 10 a.m. Eastern and 4 p.m. Central European Summer Time. Availability is explicitly limited, and Microsoft says purchases are restricted to one anniversary collection per customer.

What the $899.99 collection includes

The Xbox Series X25 Limited Edition is based on the current Series X hardware platform and includes 1 TB of storage. Its visual identity reaches back to the original 2001 Xbox: the casing is translucent green, the illuminated X motif is green, and the chassis carries a 25th-anniversary mark alongside smaller historical references.

The accompanying controller uses the same translucent OG Green theme. Microsoft has retained the original-style ABXY colors and designed the bumpers as a tribute to the black and white buttons on the original large Xbox controller commonly known as the Duke. The rear case and battery door are transparent, exposing an old-school Xbox logo beneath them.

The bundle also now includes Halo: Campaign Evolved as a digital full-game download. Microsoft describes the game as a modernized remake of the campaign that helped define the first Xbox generation. That inclusion was not part of the basic June hardware announcement and adds a software component directly tied to the anniversary theme.

Preorder dates and launch timeline

August 26: Microsoft begins sending preorder invitations to a group of selected Xbox fans. The company has not published a numerical allocation for the invitation wave.

August 27 at 7 a.m. PDT: public preorders open in select markets through Xbox and participating retailers. Regional prices and retailer lists vary, so the $899.99 figure should not be treated as a direct currency conversion for Europe or other markets.

October 20: the X25 Special Edition Wireless Controller launches as a separate product for buyers who want the anniversary design without purchasing the console bundle. Microsoft says preorders for the standalone controller are already open in select markets.

November 13: the Xbox 25th Anniversary Collection launches. The date lands two days before the 25th anniversary of the original Xbox's November 15, 2001 North American debut.

Why $899.99 is a notable price

The anniversary price arrives in a more expensive console market than the one that existed at the start of the Series X generation. In June, Microsoft announced another worldwide Xbox hardware price increase effective August 1, citing a severe rise in storage and memory costs. The company said 512 GB console prices would increase by $100 and 1 TB models by $150, while the 2 TB model would be discontinued.

That context matters. The X25 is a limited collector product, with numbered hardware and an included game, but the $899.99 price also reflects a broader increase in the cost base for console manufacturing. Microsoft said in its June pricing statement that storage and memory prices had risen by more than 2.5 times and warned that component pressure could persist.

The result is a premium anniversary machine that sits far above the traditional $499 launch-era idea of a flagship console. For collectors, the individualized numbering and nostalgic transparent shell are part of the proposition. For buyers primarily interested in performance, Microsoft has not announced an exclusive CPU, GPU or storage-performance upgrade for X25 over the underlying Series X platform.

What is confirmed, and what is not

Confirmed: the US MSRP is $899.99; the collection launches November 13; the console has 1 TB of storage; every unit is individually numbered; a download of Halo: Campaign Evolved is included; and broad preorders begin August 27 at 7 a.m. PDT in select markets.

Confirmed separately: the X25 controller launches October 20 and can be purchased without the console. The design includes transparent elements, the original ABXY color scheme and legacy-inspired bumpers.

Not yet universal: exact local prices, stock levels and participating retailers vary by market. Microsoft has not published a global production number for the console, so “limited edition” does not yet translate into a known total quantity.

No performance claim: Microsoft describes X25 as bringing the power and performance of Xbox Series X into the anniversary design. It has not presented the machine as a mid-generation performance refresh.

The anniversary campaign is bigger than one console

The console is landing inside a broader 25th-anniversary push. Xbox has introduced a profile badge, historical Game Pass collections, official merchandise, community-created themes and a FanFest tour with stops in Cologne, Tokyo, London, Mexico City, Sydney, Toronto and Seattle.

The timing is useful for Microsoft because gamescom is underway in Cologne from August 26 through August 30. Xbox is broadcasting from its booth and is using the event as a physical showcase for hardware and a large slate of upcoming games. A highly recognizable translucent console gives the anniversary campaign a visual centerpiece in a week when gaming attention is unusually concentrated.

The official Xbox reveal trailer was published June 7, 2026. It shows the translucent console and controller, including the design references to the original Xbox hardware. The video is embedded from Xbox's official YouTube channel and has not been downloaded or re-uploaded by VISIONE.

What happens next

The immediate test is availability. Because Microsoft is combining an invitation phase with a one-per-customer public preorder limit, the company is signaling that demand management matters. The first useful data point will be how quickly allocations disappear in major markets after public orders open on August 27.

Regional pricing is the second thing to watch. Microsoft says the MSRP varies by market, and recent worldwide hardware increases mean European and other local prices may diverge materially from a simple conversion of $899.99.

Finally, the bundle arrives just as Xbox leans heavily into a “return” narrative built around anniversary nostalgia, first-party games and platform identity. The X25 does not change what a Series X can do, but it is designed to make the hardware itself part of that story.

Bottom line

The Xbox Series X25 Limited Edition is now a fully specified retail product rather than a showcase tease: $899.99 in the US, public preorders from August 27, a November 13 launch, numbered translucent-green hardware and Halo: Campaign Evolved included. The premium price will narrow its audience, but Microsoft is clearly selling scarcity, history and physical design as much as raw console capability.
