EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Speed without shortcuts.

VISIONE Wire is a pilot publication focused on technology, artificial intelligence and digital business. Our goal is to publish quickly while keeping sourcing, attribution and corrections visible.

Originality

We do not publish copied articles or mass-generated rewrites. Each published report must add original structure, context, synthesis, verification or reporting beyond the source material.

Sources and attribution

Claims that depend on external reporting, company announcements, research, public records or other sources should be linked or clearly attributed. Primary sources are preferred whenever practical.

Use of AI

AI tools may assist research, transcription, structure, translation, drafting and editing. They must not be treated as a source. Material factual claims must be checked against reliable sources before publication. AI-generated text is reviewed before publication.

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected promptly. When a correction changes the meaning of a published report, the article should disclose the correction and, where useful, its date.

Headlines

Headlines should accurately represent the article. We avoid fabricated certainty, deceptive clickbait and headlines that materially overstate what sources support.

Conflicts and sponsored content

Commercial relationships that could reasonably affect reader interpretation should be disclosed. Sponsored or paid material must be clearly labeled and kept distinct from independent editorial reporting.

Publication status

VISIONE Wire is currently an experimental news lab. Its first public pages are testing technical publishing infrastructure and editorial workflows before regular publication begins.
