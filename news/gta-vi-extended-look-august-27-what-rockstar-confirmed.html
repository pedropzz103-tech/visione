GTA VI extended look is set for August 27: what Rockstar has confirmed

Rockstar has put a date and time on its next major GTA VI showcase, giving the most anticipated game on the calendar another enormous search moment before launch.

By VISIONE Wire · August 19, 2026 · 19:30 CEST

Rockstar Games has scheduled an extended look at Grand Theft Auto VI for August 27 at 3 PM ET, setting up one of the biggest gaming showcases of the month.

The date and time are now listed on Rockstar’s official GTA VI page, which also confirms that the game is due to launch on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not published a full rundown of what the August 27 presentation will contain, so the safest expectation is exactly what the company has promised: a longer look at the game rather than a specific guarantee of new gameplay systems, map details or story revelations.

Why the August 27 event matters

GTA VI is already operating at a scale where even a small update can dominate gaming search traffic. Rockstar’s current material centers on Jason and Lucia, whose story unfolds across Leonida and Vice City after a job goes wrong and pulls them into a broader criminal conspiracy.

The company has also opened pre-orders and is marketing multiple editions, including an Ultimate Edition and a Vice City-themed pre-order bonus package. The August presentation lands just days after gamescom Opening Night Live, creating an unusually dense week of major gaming announcements.

Rockstar’s official site currently says the game “plays best on PlayStation 5,” while still listing both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S as launch platforms.

What Rockstar has actually confirmed

  • Extended look: August 27, 2026
  • Time: 3 PM ET
  • Release date: November 19, 2026
  • Launch platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S
  • Pre-orders are open

Anything beyond that remains speculation until Rockstar shows it. That distinction matters with a game this heavily followed, because rumors around GTA VI can travel faster than official information.

Expect the internet to stop for a while

The practical reason to watch the August 27 showcase is simple: GTA VI is likely to become one of the largest entertainment launches of 2026. Every new trailer, screenshot and confirmed mechanic has the potential to trigger secondary waves of searches around characters, vehicles, locations, editions and release timing.

VISIONE Wire will update this report when the presentation goes live and separate confirmed details from whatever the rumor mill manages to invent in the meantime.
