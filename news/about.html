ABOUT

Reporting for the systems changing how the world works.

VISIONE Wire is an independent digital publication focused on artificial intelligence, software, computing infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital business and technology policy. The goal is not to publish every announcement. It is to explain the developments that materially change how technology is built, financed, governed or used.

Articles are selected for durable reader value. A story should leave the reader with more than the source announcement: a clearer timeline, a useful comparison, primary-document analysis, transparent calculations, technical context or a well-supported interpretation that connects several verified facts.

What makes a story worth publishing

The publication favors frontier-model developments with operational consequences, agent security and safety, chips and compute, data centers, cloud and developer infrastructure, major technology-company economics, cybersecurity incidents, competition law and technology regulation. Science, space and markets are covered when the technology or infrastructure consequences are substantial.

Routine entertainment updates, celebrity news, weak rumors, keyword-first pages and low-information product announcements are not part of the normal publishing pipeline. When there is no strong story, publishing nothing is better than filling a quota.

Ownership and responsibility

VISIONE Wire is operated by VISIONE as an independent editorial project. Pedro is the editor responsible for story selection, source review, corrections and the publication's editorial workflow.

VISIONE Wire is not affiliated with the companies, governments, regulators, media organizations or institutions it covers. Advertising does not provide editorial approval rights. Paid material, if used, must be clearly labeled and separated from independent reporting.

How we work

Primary sources are preferred whenever practical, including regulatory filings, court documents, company newsrooms, technical documentation, research papers, official datasets and public-agency material. Independent reporting is used to confirm events, add context and test claims made by interested parties.

AI tools may assist with research organization, drafting, translation and editing, but AI is not treated as a factual source. Claims are checked against external evidence before publication. More detail is available in the Editorial Standards and Accountability & Transparency pages.
