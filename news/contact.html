Tips, corrections, rights questions and feedback.

VISIONE Wire welcomes correction requests, source material, reader feedback, rights questions, advertising inquiries and general editorial messages. The fastest way to reach the newsroom is email.

Correction requests

When requesting a correction, include the article URL, the exact statement you believe is inaccurate, why it is inaccurate and a reliable source or document supporting the correction. If the correction is material, the article should be updated with a visible note or a revised modified date depending on the nature of the change.

News tips and source material

For news tips, include links to original documents, official announcements, filings, screenshots only when necessary, event pages, product pages or other verifiable material. VISIONE Wire prefers primary sources and will not treat anonymous or viral claims as confirmed without additional evidence.

Image and rights inquiries

If you believe an image, quote, logo or other material has been credited incorrectly or should be removed, send the page URL and explain the rights issue. We review rights questions and will update attribution, replace material or remove it when appropriate.

Advertising and sponsorship

Advertising questions can also be sent to this address. Sponsored material, affiliate relationships or paid placements must be clearly labeled and kept separate from independent editorial coverage. See the Advertising & Sponsorship Standards page for more detail.

Reader feedback

Readers can also send feedback about navigation, readability, broken links, missing context or coverage suggestions. VISIONE Wire is a developing publication, and feedback that improves clarity or user experience is treated as part of the editorial product, not as background noise.
