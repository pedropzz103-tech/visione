Outer Banks Season 5 premieres August 20: final season, story and what to know

The Pogues return tomorrow for one last season, with revenge, the fallout from JJ’s death and nearly 200 million views of audience history behind the finale.

By VISIONE Wire · August 19, 2026 · 19:34 CEST

Outer Banks Season 5 premieres on Netflix on August 20, bringing the Pogues back for the fifth and final chapter of one of the streamer’s most durable young-adult hits.

Netflix says the new season picks up after the death of JJ and the loss of the Blue Crown, with John B, Sarah, Kiara, Pope and Cleo pushed into a revenge story while Chandler Groff remains at large. Rafe is also drawn into an uneasy alliance with the group as the final hunt unfolds.

A finale with a very large built-in audience

Netflix says the first four seasons of Outer Banks have generated nearly 200 million views and appeared on the company’s English TV Top 10 list 25 times. That gives the final-season launch a ready-made global audience and makes August 20 one of the more important streaming dates of the month.

The creators have said for years that the story was conceived as a five-season journey, and Netflix’s Tudum coverage notes that the creative team has long known what the final scene would be.

What happens in Season 5?

The official setup has the Pogues at their breaking point after events in Morocco. They are grieving, far from home and still dealing with threats from Groff, Dalia and the Corsairs, while the Kooks are trying to make sure there is no easy homecoming.

Netflix frames the final run around revenge, redemption and the group’s attempt to reclaim a future that has repeatedly slipped out of reach.

Netflix is already feeding the hype

The streamer has released trailers, new photography and even the first eight minutes of the premiere ahead of launch. Returning regulars include Chase Stokes, Madelyn Cline, Madison Bailey, Jonathan Daviss, Drew Starkey, Carlacia Grant, Austin North and Fiona Palomo.

The timing is ideal for a search spike: fans will be looking for release times, episode details, cast information, ending explanations and character outcomes as soon as the season becomes available.
