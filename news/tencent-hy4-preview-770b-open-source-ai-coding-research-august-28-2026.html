Tencent releases Hy4 preview, a 770B open-source AI model for coding and research

The Chinese technology group is pushing a much larger Mixture-of-Experts model into the open-source race, pairing 770 billion total parameters with 49 billion active parameters, a one-million-token context window and a productivity-first pitch.

By VISIONE Wire · Published August 28, 2026 · 20:00 CEST

Tencent released and open-sourced Hy4 preview on Friday, introducing a 770-billion-parameter artificial-intelligence model designed around software engineering, office work, financial analysis, game development and scientific research. The model uses a Mixture-of-Experts architecture, so only a fraction of the network is active for each token: Tencent says 49 billion parameters are activated at a time, while the full model contains 770 billion parameters in its backbone.

The release is notable for more than raw scale. Tencent says Hy4 preview supports a context window exceeding one million tokens, is distributed under the Apache 2.0 license and can be accessed through its WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy products, Yuanbao and ima, as well as through APIs offered by Tencent Cloud TokenHub and OpenRouter. Model weights are available on Hugging Face, including an FP8 version.

That puts Hy4 into a crowded but strategically important part of the AI market: large open-weight models that companies and researchers can inspect, adapt and deploy without depending entirely on a closed consumer chatbot. Tencent is positioning the model not as a general-purpose demo first, but as infrastructure for work.

What Tencent actually released

The technical model card describes Hy4 preview as a 78-layer Mixture-of-Experts system. The first layer uses a standard feed-forward network, while the remaining 77 layers use routed experts. Each of those MoE layers contains 256 routed experts plus one shared expert, and each token activates the top eight routed experts alongside the shared expert.

Tencent also includes a native multi-token-prediction layer intended to support speculative decoding. The company says the model uses Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention with an IndexCache mechanism for reusing sparse indexes across layers, as well as identity Hyper-Connections in the residual pathway. Those architectural details matter because a 770B model would be extraordinarily expensive to run if all of its parameters were active on every token. MoE systems trade a larger total parameter pool for a much smaller active compute footprint.

The one-million-token context window is another major part of the pitch. In practical terms, long-context systems are designed to work across large codebases, extensive document collections, research material or multi-file projects without forcing users to compress everything into a short prompt. Whether Hy4 maintains accuracy across the full advertised window will require independent testing.

Coding, spreadsheets, games and research

Tencent says the model was trained and evaluated around real productivity work with input from internal software engineers, game developers, finance analysts and security specialists. The company highlights stronger planning, debugging and validation for long-horizon software tasks, along with improvements in front-end generation and interaction quality.

For office work, Tencent says Hy4 can operate across messy multi-file contexts and produce documents, spreadsheets and presentations while handling equations, data analysis and financial models. In game development, the company claims the model can turn a single natural-language instruction into a playable prototype and continue refining projects across multiple interactions with game engines.

Scientific research is another target. Tencent lists AI research, molecular dynamics, condensed-matter physics and pure mathematics among the areas where it observed gains. These are company claims rather than independent conclusions, and the model is explicitly labeled a preview.

Tencent's internal benchmark claims

Tencent published results from a blind internal evaluation involving 163 experts and 203 engineering tasks. In that test, Hy4 preview received an average score of 2.99 out of 4.00. Tencent says that was slightly ahead of GLM 5.3 at 2.92 and Kimi K3 at 2.94.

The company also reported pairwise results. Against GLM 5.3, Hy4 recorded a 46.8% win rate, 12.8% ties and 40.4% losses. Against Kimi K3, Tencent reported 51.2% wins, 7.9% ties and 40.9% losses.

Those numbers are useful as a description of Tencent's own evaluation, but they should not be read as a definitive independent ranking. The test set, judges and methodology were controlled internally, and model performance can change sharply depending on prompts, agent scaffolding, tool access, hardware and workload.

A model that helped optimize itself

One of Tencent's more unusual claims is that Hy4 preview participated in parts of its own development process. The company says the model proposed approaches, ran experiments and iterated on training methods, data strategies, evaluation frameworks and low-level operators, with resulting code and feedback feeding into later rounds of work.

Tencent describes that as an early-stage recursive self-improvement loop. The phrase is easy to overread. The company is not saying the model autonomously redesigned and retrained itself without human infrastructure or oversight. It is saying the model was used as an engineering agent inside the research process.

The company also says Hy4 analyzed bottlenecks in its inference stack and helped optimize operator fusion and communication, producing a 31.8% increase in end-to-end throughput against Tencent's baseline. As with the benchmark results, that figure is vendor-reported and awaits broader reproduction.

Open source and pricing

The Hugging Face release carries an Apache 2.0 license, one of the most permissive standard open-source licenses used in software and AI releases. That allows broad use, modification and redistribution subject to the license terms and notices.

Tencent is simultaneously offering commercial API access. It lists pricing at $0.834 per million input tokens, $2.501 per million output tokens and $0.042 per million cached tokens. The company says Hy4 preview will be free through WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy for two weeks after launch, while free access to the previous Hy3 model on those products has been extended through September 30.

Tencent Cloud's TokenHub documentation also lists Hy4 preview as compatible with OpenAI Chat Completions, OpenAI Responses and Anthropic Messages protocols. That compatibility can reduce switching costs for developers because existing SDKs and clients can often connect with relatively small changes.

Known limitations

Tencent is unusually direct about several weaknesses in the preview. The model card says Hy4 can spend longer than necessary reasoning through difficult tasks and can over-verify its own work. The company says there is still meaningful room for improvement in both pre-training and post-training.

Those caveats are important. Very large reasoning models can look impressive on benchmark suites while still being inefficient, inconsistent or unnecessarily verbose in real deployment. For developers, the relevant question will be not only whether Hy4 solves a task, but how much compute, latency and orchestration it needs to do so reliably.

Why this release matters

Hy4 preview expands the competition among Chinese AI labs and technology companies building open-weight frontier systems. Its size places it among the largest publicly released MoE models, but the more strategically important details are the one-million-token context, permissive licensing, API compatibility and explicit focus on productive agent-style work.

The release also shows how quickly the boundary between a model and a software platform is dissolving. Tencent is training Hy4 with feedback from its own products, deploying it inside those products, exposing it through standard APIs and using the model to help optimize the infrastructure on which it runs. That feedback loop is becoming a core advantage for companies that own both models and large software ecosystems.

What comes next

Tencent says additional models in the Hy4 family are expected soon. Independent evaluations will now matter more than launch-day charts: developers will want to know how the model performs on long-context retrieval, agentic coding, tool use, multilingual work, hallucination resistance and real inference cost across different hardware setups.

For now, the confirmed story is substantial on its own. Tencent has placed a 770B-parameter, 49B-active MoE model into the open-source ecosystem, attached a one-million-token context window and Apache 2.0 license, and made productivity rather than chatbot conversation the center of its pitch.
