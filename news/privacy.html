Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026.

VISIONE Wire is operated by VISIONE. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the publication, contact the newsroom or interact with advertising and embedded services.

Information we may receive

Like most websites, hosting, domain, security and infrastructure providers may process technical information required to deliver the site, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, referrer information and security logs.

If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, message content, source material, correction request or rights inquiry. Do not send confidential material unless you understand the risks of ordinary email.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate the site, respond to messages, review correction requests, handle rights questions, protect the site from abuse, understand technical problems, comply with legal obligations and maintain basic business records.

Advertising and measurement

VISIONE Wire uses Google AdSense to support the publication. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers or similar technologies for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, measurement and, where consent permits, personalization.

European visitors

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, VISIONE Wire uses Google's certified consent management platform to collect and communicate applicable advertising consent choices when required. Consent choices may affect whether ads are personalized or non-personalized.

External links, images and embeds

Articles may link to third-party websites and may display images or videos hosted by third parties such as official newsrooms, government media libraries, Wikimedia Commons, YouTube or company press pages. Those services may receive standard request information when your browser loads their resources and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

We retain direct correspondence only as reasonably necessary for editorial correspondence, corrections, rights handling, security, legal obligations or business operations. No method of internet transmission is completely secure, and no website can guarantee absolute protection.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted directly to us. Privacy rights differ by jurisdiction and may include access, correction, deletion, objection or restriction rights.

Children

VISIONE Wire is a general news, technology and culture publication and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account creation because the site does not offer reader accounts.
