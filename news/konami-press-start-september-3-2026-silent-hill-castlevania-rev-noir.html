Konami Press Start September 3, 2026: Silent Hill, Castlevania, Rev. NOiR and how to watch

Konami has set a worldwide publisher broadcast for September 3 with new gameplay, developer messages and exclusive footage. Silent Hill: Townfall, Castlevania: Belmont's Curse and Rev. NOiR are confirmed, while the company has explicitly ruled out news about the Silent Hill remake.

By VISIONE Wire · September 1, 2026 · 18:02 CEST

Konami will hold a new KONAMI PRESS START broadcast on September 3, 2026 at 8:00 a.m. PDT, with Silent Hill: Townfall, Castlevania: Belmont's Curse and the recently announced RPG Rev. NOiR leading the confirmed lineup. The publisher says the program will stream worldwide and include new gameplay footage, video messages from developers and footage that will be exclusive to the broadcast.

The event lands in an unusually crowded week for game showcases. Sony has already scheduled two back-to-back State of Play broadcasts for the same day, putting Konami's show into a concentrated window for major September announcements. That timing gives the presentation strong search interest, but the confirmed list is already substantial enough that speculation is unnecessary.

What is confirmed: KONAMI PRESS START begins September 3 at 8:00 a.m. PDT / 11:00 a.m. EDT / 4:00 p.m. BST / 5:00 p.m. CEST. Konami says Silent Hill: Townfall, Castlevania: Belmont's Curse and Rev. NOiR will appear, alongside other current and upcoming titles. The show is planned for about 30 minutes. Konami has explicitly said there will be no announcements regarding a remake of Silent Hill.

When Konami Press Start starts

Konami's official announcement gives the start time as 8:00 a.m. PDT on September 3. That converts to 11:00 a.m. on the U.S. East Coast, 4:00 p.m. in the United Kingdom and 5:00 p.m. in Central Europe. In Japan, the broadcast begins at midnight on September 4.

The publisher's dedicated Press Start page says the runtime is planned at approximately 30 minutes, while also warning that the streaming time may change without prior notice. The presentation will include games intended for mature audiences.

Silent Hill: Townfall is the clearest near-term focus

Silent Hill: Townfall is one of the most important confirmed games because it is also the closest to release. Konami's September 1 announcement lists the game for September 24, giving the publisher a narrow window to show final gameplay, clarify launch details and explain how the project fits into the wider Silent Hill lineup.

Konami says new gameplay will be shown during Press Start. That is a factual promise from the publisher; what the footage will reveal about combat, exploration, story structure or performance remains unknown until the broadcast airs.

The company has also taken the unusual step of drawing a hard line around a separate project. Its announcement states that there will be no information about a Silent Hill remake. That makes any claim that the show is secretly built around such an announcement unsupported at this point.

Castlevania: Belmont's Curse arrives October 15

Castlevania: Belmont's Curse is the second major release highlighted by Konami. The publisher lists an October 15 release date and says Press Start will include fresh material from the game.

The timing is notable because September 2026 marks Castlevania's 40th anniversary. Konami's own release describes the franchise as dating back to 1986 and emphasizes its long-running role in action-adventure gaming. That anniversary gives the September broadcast additional weight even without an unannounced title.

What Konami has not promised is a broader anniversary roadmap. New collections, remakes or surprise projects remain speculation unless the company says otherwise during the event.

Rev. NOiR gives Konami a newer property to push

Rev. NOiR, described by Konami as a recently announced RPG, is also confirmed. Unlike Silent Hill and Castlevania, it does not carry decades of franchise history, so the broadcast could serve as an important introduction for players who only saw the initial reveal.

Konami's official materials promise developer messages and new footage across the lineup, but they do not specify exactly how the 30 minutes will be divided. That leaves open whether Rev. NOiR receives a short update or a more substantial gameplay segment.

More games are expected, but the exact list is still fluid

The official Press Start page describes the show as covering both new and upcoming titles as well as active live games. Secondary reporting and Konami's event materials also point to additional titles such as eFootball-related content and other projects, but VISIONE Wire is treating only the publisher's directly confirmed names as the core lineup until the stream begins.

This distinction matters because publisher showcases often generate long speculative lists before they air. Search demand can reward dramatic predictions, but a useful preview should separate what viewers are guaranteed to see from what fans merely hope to see.

How to watch the official stream

Konami says the broadcast will be available through its official YouTube, Twitch and other publisher channels. The dedicated Press Start page already links an official YouTube stream. Viewers can open the original stream directly at Konami's official YouTube broadcast.

Konami also says fan restreams are permitted using the #KonamiPressStart hashtag. VISIONE Wire is linking the official stream rather than re-uploading or mirroring the video.

Why September 3 is becoming a major games-news day

Press Start will not happen in isolation. Sony's previously announced State of Play and State of Play Japan are scheduled on September 3 as well. For players, that creates a compact sequence of announcements from two major publishers. For studios and platforms, it creates a crowded attention market where trailers, release dates and gameplay reveals will compete within hours of one another.

That context also means individual announcements may move quickly. Release dates, platforms and feature lists shown during the broadcast should be checked against the final publisher materials rather than inferred from teaser language.

What to watch for

The strongest confirmed questions are straightforward. How much new gameplay will Silent Hill: Townfall receive three weeks before launch? Will Castlevania: Belmont's Curse use its 40th-anniversary timing to reveal additional modes or launch details? How much of Rev. NOiR's systems and world will Konami finally explain? And which of the publisher's active games will receive meaningful updates rather than short montage appearances?

Those are questions the broadcast can answer. Claims about surprise remakes or unannounced sequels cannot be treated as fact before the show, especially when Konami has already ruled out one of the most obvious rumors.

Bottom line

KONAMI PRESS START is shaping up as a compact but important September showcase rather than a mystery-box event. The publisher has already confirmed three significant games, a roughly 30-minute runtime, new gameplay and developer messages. The most useful expectation is therefore not to chase hypothetical surprises, but to watch for concrete release information and substantial footage from games that are already close to market.

The show begins September 3 at 8:00 a.m. PDT, with the official stream available through Konami's channels. VISIONE Wire will treat any post-show updates as new facts only once they are confirmed in the broadcast or subsequent publisher materials.
