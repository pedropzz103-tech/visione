Marvel’s Wolverine launches September 15: price, gameplay, accessibility and pre-order details

Insomniac’s brutal single-player Wolverine game is one month from launch, with a September 15 date, extensive accessibility support and pre-orders already live.

By VISIONE Wire · August 19, 2026 · 19:38 CEST

Marvel’s Wolverine launches September 15, 2026 on PlayStation 5, putting Insomniac Games’ next Marvel title less than a month away.

PlayStation lists the standard edition at $69.99 in the United States and €79.99 in Europe, with a Digital Deluxe Edition priced higher and adding cosmetic suits, claws and extra technique points. Pre-orders are already open.

A deliberately brutal Wolverine game

Insomniac describes the game as a violent, action-heavy single-player adventure built around James “Logan” Howlett. The story follows Wolverine as he reconnects with Team X and fights Reavers while mutants are being hunted and captured.

Jean Grey appears as a major ally, and Bolivar Trask sits behind the Reavers’ operation. Gameplay shown by PlayStation includes stealth, ambushes, vehicle sequences, special techniques, critical strikes, Wolverine’s Rage system and his Healing Factor.

Accessibility is a major launch feature

PlayStation says the game will ship with a broad set of accessibility options aimed at players with cognitive, visual, auditory and motor disabilities. The PlayStation Store currently lists 35 accessibility features.

The game is also marked as PS5 Pro Enhanced, supports Remote Play, Game Help, DualSense vibration and trigger effects, and is designed for one-player offline play.

Pre-order bonuses

Confirmed pre-order bonuses include early access to the Classic Brown suit, Reflective Claws, one additional Technique Point and four PlayStation avatars. Insomniac stresses that the paid-edition suit and claw options are cosmetic.

The release window gives Sony a major exclusive heading into the final stretch of 2026, and search interest should accelerate as reviews, performance comparisons, trophy information and story details begin appearing closer to launch.
