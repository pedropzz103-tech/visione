# VISIONE Institutional Redesign

## Goal

Replace the temporary coming-soon screen with a polished, responsive institutional website that presents VISIONE as a technology studio and directs editorial traffic to `wire.visione.one`.

## Audience and primary action

The site serves founders and businesses that need a digital product, website, SaaS platform, integration, automation, or technical execution for an idea. The primary action is starting a project conversation through email.

## Information architecture

The single-page site contains a compact fixed navigation, a clear hero, a service overview, a process section, an outcomes-focused capability panel, an about statement, and a final contact call to action. “Wire” appears as a distinct external editorial destination rather than a service or content feed on the institutional home.

## Visual direction

The experience uses a bright white and cool-gray canvas, the existing blue/cyan/violet VISIONE palette, dark navy typography, restrained translucent surfaces, subtle grid geometry, and asymmetric editorial composition. The existing logo remains the primary brand asset. Yellow is prohibited.

## Content and interaction

- Language: English, consistent with the existing brand slogan.
- Services: websites, digital products, SaaS, web services/APIs, automation/integrations, and idea development.
- Navigation: in-page anchors plus external Wire link and a prominent contact action.
- Contact: `mailto:` action, with no invented form backend.
- Motion: restrained reveal and ambient effects, disabled when reduced motion is preferred.
- Responsive: usable from small mobile screens through desktop, with accessible navigation and readable type.

## Technical constraints

Keep the existing static HTML/CSS architecture, logo file, `CNAME`, and GitHub Pages compatibility. Add no runtime dependency and no framework migration. Tests validate required page structure, external Wire destination, contact action, identity preservation, and the absence of yellow design tokens.
