Gamescom Award 2026 nominees: Metro 2039 leads, Alien: Isolation 2 and Denshattack! follow

The official shortlist puts 4A Games' Metro 2039 at the front with five nominations, while Sega-linked titles dominate the broader field ahead of public voting and the August 28 awards show.

By VISIONE Wire · Published August 25, 2026 · 12:01 CEST

The official Gamescom Award 2026 nominees have been announced, and Metro 2039 enters the event as the most-nominated game. The title from 4A Games and Deep Silver received five nominations, including Best Visuals and Most Epic, plus nominations across every platform category for which it is eligible.

Alien: Isolation 2 from Creative Assembly and Sega and indie title Denshattack! from Undercoders and Fireshine Games follow with four nominations each. The organizer says Sega-published games collected eight nominations overall once Alien: Isolation 2, Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000 and STRANGER THAN HEAVEN are counted together.

The nominee list was published by gamescom on August 24, one day before Opening Night Live formally opens the public-facing news cycle in Cologne. It is a useful snapshot of which playable games have made the strongest early impression on the event's international jury, but it is not the same thing as a final ranking: winners will be determined later in the week, with community voting contributing in a number of categories.

Metro 2039 leads with five nominations

Metro 2039 is nominated for Best Visuals and Most Epic, and it also appears in the Best Microsoft Xbox Game, Best PC Game and Best Sony PlayStation Game categories. That spread matters because it places the title in both craft-oriented judging and platform-specific competition.

The five-nomination total gives 4A Games and Deep Silver the clearest headline going into the show. It also reflects the unusual breadth of Metro 2039's presence at gamescom: rather than appearing in one or two specialist categories, it is competing across presentation, scale and platform groups.

Alien: Isolation 2 and Denshattack! each earn four

Alien: Isolation 2 is nominated for Best Visuals, Best Audio, Best Gameplay and Best PC Game. That concentration is notable because three of those categories are direct evaluations of the experience itself rather than broader marketing or booth awards. Creative Assembly therefore enters the event with the sequel recognized across visual design, sound and gameplay.

Denshattack!, meanwhile, shows the opposite kind of strength: an indie title breaking into multiple categories against much larger publishers. It is nominated for Best Audio, Best Gameplay, Most Entertaining and Best Nintendo Switch 2 Game. Gamescom notes that the title was announced during last year's Opening Night Live, giving it a neat one-year arc from reveal to major award contender.

Sega has the broadest publisher footprint

Gamescom says Sega titles account for eight nominations in total. Alien: Isolation 2 supplies four, while Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000 receives nominations for Most Epic and Best PC Game. STRANGER THAN HEAVEN from RGG Studio is nominated for Most Epic and Best Microsoft Xbox Game.

That does not make Sega the winner of any category yet. It does, however, give the publisher one of the strongest portfolio-level positions in the official shortlist. The Global awards also include a Best Lineup category judged across an exhibitor's overall portfolio, though gamescom has not published a conventional nominee list for all of the jury-only Global categories.

The biggest categories and contenders

Best Visuals includes Alien: Isolation 2, DEPERSON, Forza Horizon 6, Inkblood and Metro 2039. Best Audio includes Alien: Isolation 2, Cairn, Denshattack!, Forza Horizon 6 and There are No Ghosts at the Grand.

For Best Gameplay, the nominees are Alien: Isolation 2, Cairn, Denshattack!, Onimusha: Way of the Sword and The Blood of Dawnwalker. The Most Epic field includes Crimson Desert, Metro 2039, STRANGER THAN HEAVEN, Tides of Annihilation and Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000.

The platform categories create another layer of competition. Best Microsoft Xbox Game includes Forza Horizon 6, Gears of War: E-Day, Halo: Campaign Evolved, Metro 2039 and STRANGER THAN HEAVEN. Best PC Game includes Alien: Isolation 2, Crimson Desert, Guns of Eschaton, Metro 2039 and Total War: WARHAMMER 40,000.

On PlayStation, Dragon's Dogma 2 Dark Arisen, Metro 2039, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, The Blood of Dawnwalker and Tides of Annihilation are nominated. The Nintendo Switch 2 category includes Denshattack!, Mega Man: Dual Override, Nintendo Switch Sport Resort, OPUS: Prism Peak and Rayman Legends Retold.

When public voting opens

Fans and visitors can vote from Wednesday, August 26, until Thursday, August 27 at 2:00 PM CEST. Gamescom says voting will be available both online through its official website and on site through QR codes.

Community voting applies to the Arts and Platform groups as well as Best Booth (Consumer Award), Best Merch and Best Trailer / Announcement. The final category is especially fluid because all trailers and game announcements shown during Opening Night Live are eligible, meaning its effective field is not complete until the broadcast has taken place.

When the winners will be announced

The winners are scheduled to be announced during the Gamescom Award show on Friday, August 28, from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM CEST at the gamescom studio stage in Hall 7. The result combines jury input with audience participation for the categories where public voting applies.

The awards operate across 19 categories split into Arts, Platform and Global groups. Some Global awards are decided by the jury, including Best Lineup, Best Booth (Jury Award), Best Business Booth and the special HEART OF GAMING award.

What the nominations do and do not tell us

The shortlist is an editorial signal, not a commercial forecast. Gamescom's judging criteria include elements such as visual quality, audio, gameplay, innovation, entertainment value, technological progress and sales prospects, but a nomination does not guarantee critical consensus once games launch or become widely playable.

It is also important that the eligible field is tied to games presented at gamescom and submitted for consideration. The list therefore should not be read as a ranking of every major game in development during 2026. It reflects the event's own competitive pool.

Still, the nominations matter because gamescom is one of the largest gaming events in the world and because the shortlist lands at the exact moment publishers are competing for attention. Metro 2039's five nominations, Alien: Isolation 2's craft-heavy showing and Denshattack!'s indie breakout status give all three titles a stronger narrative heading into hands-on coverage and post-show searches.

The bottom line

Metro 2039 is the early leader of the Gamescom Award 2026 field with five nominations. Alien: Isolation 2 and Denshattack! have four each, and Sega-linked games account for eight nominations overall. Public voting opens August 26 and closes August 27 at 2 PM CEST, with winners announced August 28.

The next material update will come from the event itself: Opening Night Live can add new trailers and announcements to the consumer-voted Global categories, while hands-on impressions from the Cologne show floor may change how the nominated games are perceived before the trophies are handed out.
