EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Fast, clear and accountable.

VISIONE Wire covers global news, business, markets, technology, artificial intelligence, gaming, entertainment, policy and culture. Our aim is to publish quickly without lowering standards for sourcing, attribution, clarity, image use, user experience or corrections.

Originality and value

We do not publish copied articles, mass-generated rewrites or pages created only to target keywords. Every report should add original organization, synthesis, comparison, context, timeline, source separation, practical explanation or analysis beyond the source material.

A report should answer the reader's real questions: what happened, who confirmed it, what changed, what remains uncertain, what happens next and why it matters. If there is not enough reliable information to answer those questions, the story should be held, shortened as a brief update or skipped.

Sources and attribution

Claims that depend on external reporting, company announcements, court filings, research, public records, regulatory documents, official stores or other outside information should be linked or clearly attributed. Primary sources are preferred whenever practical, especially for dates, prices, specifications, legal findings, earnings numbers and official statements.

Secondary reporting is used when it adds independent confirmation, reaction, market context or material details that a primary source does not provide. VISIONE Wire does not treat a viral post, rumor or anonymous claim as confirmed fact unless it is supported by stronger evidence.

Fact, source claim and analysis

Articles should distinguish between confirmed facts, statements made by a source, reported information and VISIONE Wire analysis. Company benchmarks, government claims and litigation allegations are not automatically treated as independently proven. The article should tell readers where the claim comes from.

Use of AI

AI tools may assist with research support, transcription, translation, drafting and editing. They are not treated as a source. Factual claims must be checked against reliable information before publication, and AI-assisted output is reviewed before publishing.

AI assistance does not replace editorial judgment. It must not be used to invent names, quotes, credentials, images, citations, dates, prices, specifications or legal conclusions. When a detail is not known, the article should say that it is not known.

Images

Each new or updated article should actively look for a relevant image from a safe source: official press material, company newsrooms, government media libraries, event press kits, Wikimedia Commons with a compatible license, public-domain repositories or VISIONE's own hosted assets. Images should include descriptive alt text and credit or license information when required.

We avoid unlicensed fan art, random hotlinked images, compressed thumbnails, screenshots used as decoration, AI-generated images that are not clearly identified and images that create a misleading impression of the story.

Video and embeds

When official video improves an article, VISIONE Wire should embed the official player or link to the original source. We do not download and re-upload third-party video. If a video identifier or source cannot be verified, the article should link to the official page or publish without video.

Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected promptly. When a correction changes the meaning of a report, the article should disclose that correction and, when useful, the time or date it was made. Minor formatting, grammar, broken-link or typographical fixes may be made without a formal note.

Updates to existing articles

Ongoing stories may be updated on the same URL when that is better for readers and for continuity. A substantial update should show a modified date, and the homepage may mark the story as updated rather than creating a duplicate card.

Headlines

Headlines should accurately reflect the article. We avoid misleading certainty, deceptive clickbait and wording that materially overstates what the reporting supports. Search visibility matters, but a search-friendly headline still has to be true.

Commercial separation

Sponsored or paid material must be clearly labeled and kept distinct from independent editorial work. Advertisers do not receive the right to approve or rewrite independent editorial coverage. See our Advertising & Sponsorship Standards.

Scope

VISIONE Wire is a broad digital publication rather than a narrow vertical. Coverage can move across news, markets, technology, games, entertainment, policy, business and culture whenever the story justifies it. The Coverage Areas page explains that selection logic in more detail.
