OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads hits $1 billion annualized run rate as self-service expands globally

The advertising business crossed the milestone less than 200 days after launch, according to OpenAI. Self-service buying is now expanding across India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa as the company builds a second major monetization engine around ChatGPT.

By VISIONE Wire · August 31, 2026 · 20:01 CEST

OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads has reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch, giving the company a fast-growing advertising business alongside subscriptions, enterprise software and API usage. The company announced the milestone on August 31 and said advertisers will be able to buy ChatGPT ads directly through Ads Manager across India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa starting later the same day.

The headline number is significant, but it needs to be read precisely. A $1 billion annualized run rate is not the same thing as $1 billion of advertising revenue already collected over a full year. It is a snapshot of the current pace of the business, projected over twelve months. That distinction matters for a product that has been scaling rapidly and entering new countries in waves.

What is confirmed: OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in less than 200 days, is used by tens of thousands of advertisers, is available in more than 40 countries through sales and partners, and is opening self-service Ads Manager access across India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa. These figures come from OpenAI and have not been independently audited in the announcement.

From a U.S. test to a global ad platform

OpenAI began testing ads inside ChatGPT in the United States earlier in 2026. The initial rollout focused on the Free and Go plans, while Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts remained ad-free. OpenAI has framed advertising as a way to support wider access to ChatGPT without making subscription revenue the only way to finance the service.

By August, the system had moved beyond an experimental placement layer. OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads is now available in more than 40 countries through its Ads Solutions team, agencies and technology partners. The company also says it has built an ecosystem of more than 50 technology and measurement partners.

The addition of self-service access is strategically important because it changes the advertiser mix. Managed sales can work for large brands, but an Ads Manager allows smaller businesses to launch campaigns without a direct sales relationship. OpenAI says small and medium-sized businesses already represent a material share of the advertising business after Ads Manager debuted in May.

How ChatGPT ads are supposed to work

OpenAI says ads are clearly labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT's answers. The company also says advertisers do not receive access to private conversations and that advertising does not influence the answers users receive.

Relevance is driven first by the context of the current conversation. Depending on the country and a user's settings, OpenAI says the ad system may also use context from the person's broader ChatGPT experience. The company provides controls for ad personalization, and its Help Center says personalization rules differ by region.

That model is different from the familiar search-ad auction built around a short keyword query. In a ChatGPT conversation, a user may explain a goal, budget, constraints and preferences over multiple turns. For advertisers, that creates the possibility of reaching someone deeper in a decision process. For users and regulators, it also raises obvious questions about privacy, transparency and how conversational context is used for commercial targeting.

The $1 billion number and what it does not tell us

An annualized run rate is useful because it describes the scale the business has reached now. It is less useful as a prediction of what OpenAI will actually report in advertising revenue over the next twelve months. Campaign demand can change, expansion can accelerate or slow, and pricing may evolve as more advertisers enter the platform.

OpenAI has not disclosed in the August 31 announcement the exact monthly revenue used to calculate the run rate, average prices per click or impression, advertiser retention, gross margins, regional revenue distribution or the share of total company revenue represented by ads. Those missing details make it impossible to compare ChatGPT Ads cleanly with established advertising platforms on profitability or efficiency.

Still, crossing a billion-dollar annualized pace in less than 200 days suggests that advertising has moved from a side experiment into a meaningful business line. Reuters also reported the milestone and described it as part of OpenAI's broader effort to diversify revenue as the company expands infrastructure spending and prepares for the possibility of entering public markets.

OpenAI is building more conventional ad-tech machinery

The company says CPC and outcome-optimized bidding now account for the majority of campaigns. It has also introduced Pixel and Conversions API tools, product feeds, geographic and platform targeting, custom audiences and other features familiar to advertisers from mature digital ad systems.

Those additions matter because a large audience alone does not create a durable advertising platform. Advertisers need measurement, optimization, conversion tracking and ways to move campaign data between their own systems and the platform. OpenAI's move toward those standard tools suggests it wants ChatGPT Ads to compete for recurring marketing budgets rather than one-off experimental spending.

OpenAI highlighted two performance examples in its announcement: an ecommerce advertiser that it says achieved a three-times return on ad spend over 28 days, and a technology partner that reported more than 80% of ad-driven ChatGPT traffic came from new customers. Those are company-selected case studies, not broad performance benchmarks, so they should not be generalized to all advertisers.

Why Europe is a particularly important expansion

Opening self-service access across Europe gives OpenAI a large new advertiser base, but the region also has stricter privacy and platform rules than many other markets. The company's Help Center already notes that personalized advertising is not initially available in the European Economic Area and Switzerland in the same way it is in some other regions.

That means the international expansion will test whether conversational advertising can scale while adapting to different consent, privacy and data-use requirements. If OpenAI can maintain useful targeting with more limited personalization in Europe, it could strengthen the argument that the format does not depend entirely on extensive behavioral profiles.

Advertising becomes a fourth pillar of OpenAI's business model

OpenAI describes advertising as one part of a diversified model that also includes consumer subscriptions, enterprise products and usage-based APIs. The company says ChatGPT now serves more than one billion weekly active users, giving the ad business an enormous potential distribution surface.

The economic logic is straightforward. Free users create substantial infrastructure costs, but they also represent audience scale that can be monetized without forcing every person into a paid subscription. At the same time, OpenAI has to protect the trust that made ChatGPT valuable in the first place. If users begin to suspect answers are shaped by advertisers, the product's credibility could erode quickly.

OpenAI says it is drawing a hard line between advertising and answers. The durability of that promise will be one of the central questions as formats become more native, targeting becomes more sophisticated and advertisers push for measurable influence over purchase decisions.

What comes next

OpenAI says the next phase will bring ChatGPT Ads to more markets and add more formats, objectives, buying options and measurement capabilities. It also says it will explore more native ways for businesses to interact with consumers inside ChatGPT.

That last point could become the most consequential part of the roadmap. Traditional display ads are easy to separate visually from a chatbot response. More interactive commercial experiences will require clearer boundaries so users can distinguish assistance from paid placement at every stage.

For now, the August 31 milestone establishes that ChatGPT advertising has become a business with real scale far faster than many new ad platforms manage. The unresolved question is whether OpenAI can turn that early run rate into durable revenue while keeping user trust, regional compliance and answer independence intact. The next few quarters should provide much better evidence than the headline number alone.
