Gamescom 2026 Opening Night Live today: start time, livestream, Witcher 3 and what is confirmed

The official Opening Night Live broadcast begins today, August 25, at 8 PM CEST / 2 PM ET / 11 AM PT, kicking off gamescom 2026 before the Cologne show floor opens on August 26.

By VISIONE Wire · Published August 19, 2026 · Updated August 25, 2026 · 08:20 CEST

Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026 takes place today, Tuesday, August 25, in Cologne. The official broadcast is scheduled for 8:00 PM CEST in Germany, 2:00 PM ET and 11:00 AM PT. The stream will be hosted by Geoff Keighley and Eefje “Sjokz” Depoortere and serves as the global opening show before the main gamescom exhibition runs from August 26 through August 30.

The timing is now confirmed by the official livestream page and gamescom’s own event material. That makes today the highest-intensity search window of the week for trailers, release dates, gameplay and surprise announcements. The event organizer says the international gaming community will be focused on Cologne for the show, while the official gamescom site lists Opening Night Live on August 25 and the main public event from August 26 to 30.

Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026 start time

The official show begins at 20:00 CEST in Cologne. That converts to 19:00 BST in the UK, 14:00 ET on the US East Coast and 11:00 PT on the US West Coast. Viewers should distinguish the livestream start from gamescom’s physical exhibition dates: Opening Night Live is on August 25, while the main halls open on August 26.

Gamescom’s official website describes Opening Night Live as the opening stage for the show, and its press information confirms the event officially opens on the evening of August 25. The broader gamescom week also includes gamescom dev, the developer conference, which runs before the consumer floor fully opens.

The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past is confirmed for the show

One of the clearest confirmed appearances is The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past. Gamescom’s official exhibitor page for CD Projekt Red says viewers will get a glimpse of the expansion during Opening Night Live on August 25. The event’s product page describes Songs of the Past as a brand-new, full-fledged expansion for The Witcher 3.

The expansion is being created in partnership with Fool’s Theory, whose team includes veteran Witcher developers. Players return as Geralt of Rivia for a new adventure before the franchise moves forward with Ciri in The Witcher 4. The official gamescom listing says Songs of the Past is planned for 2027 on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

That wording is important. “Full-fledged expansion” sets a very different expectation from a small update or remaster patch, particularly because The Witcher 3’s earlier Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine expansions became reference points for large-scale post-launch RPG content. No exact 2027 launch date, final price or total playtime has been confirmed, so those remain open questions rather than facts.

What else is confirmed around gamescom 2026

The official gamescom site currently highlights a broad slate of companies and games across the event, including Nintendo, Sega, Bandai Namco, Ubisoft, CD Projekt Red and others. However, being present at gamescom does not automatically mean a title will appear in Opening Night Live. VISIONE Wire is separating confirmed ONL appearances from general show-floor participation to avoid turning exhibitor lists into speculative reveal lists.

The main event runs from August 26 through August 30 at Koelnmesse in Cologne. Gamescom’s organizers describe it as the world’s largest games event and say the 2026 edition has expanded its international and business footprint. Gamescom dev, its official developer conference, takes place around the opening of the week and is designed for industry professionals, while the public event combines playable games, publisher booths, community programming, esports and entertainment.

Why today matters for publishers and search traffic

Opening Night Live compresses a large amount of marketing into a single global broadcast. A trailer shown for two minutes can immediately generate separate searches for release date, platforms, price, gameplay, pre-orders, system requirements and developer interviews. That makes the show more than an entertainment stream: it is also a coordinated launch point for news cycles across publishers, storefronts, social platforms and gaming media.

The strongest stories will therefore be the ones backed by primary material published at the same time as the broadcast. A trailer alone can confirm footage and branding, but press releases, store pages and developer posts often carry the precise dates, platform lists and technical details that matter after the initial reveal. VISIONE Wire will treat those primary documents as the baseline when individual announcements break.

What is still uncertain

Opening Night Live is built partly around surprises, so a complete pre-show lineup is neither available nor desirable from the organizer’s perspective. Any game not explicitly announced by gamescom, its publisher or its developer should be treated as unconfirmed until it appears on the stream or receives an official post.

That also means leaks, retailer placeholders and social-media speculation should not be presented as confirmed reveals. Today’s show may produce major announcements, but the reliable line is simple: the broadcast time, hosts, event dates and Witcher appearance are confirmed; everything else needs source-by-source verification as it happens.

What happens after Opening Night Live

Gamescom’s main exhibition begins Wednesday, August 26, and continues through Sunday, August 30. For some games, the broadcast will be only the first layer. Publishers can follow a trailer with hands-on demos, developer presentations, interviews and deeper gameplay sessions on the show floor.

CD Projekt Red is following exactly that pattern with Songs of the Past. Its official gamescom material says the expansion will appear during Opening Night Live, with showcases beginning in the entertainment area from August 26. That creates a likely two-stage news cycle: the initial reveal today, followed by additional detail once demonstrations and presentations begin.

The bottom line

Gamescom 2026 effectively begins tonight for the global audience. Opening Night Live starts at 8 PM CEST / 2 PM ET / 11 AM PT on August 25, with an official YouTube livestream hosted by Geoff Keighley and Sjokz. The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past is one of the confirmed appearances, and the main Cologne exhibition opens August 26.

The highest-value details will arrive from primary sources during and immediately after the broadcast. Until then, the confirmed schedule and official stream are the useful anchors. VISIONE Wire will update individual stories as publishers release verifiable information.
