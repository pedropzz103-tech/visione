EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Speed without shortcuts.

VISIONE Wire covers technology, artificial intelligence and digital business. Our goal is to publish quickly while keeping sourcing, attribution and corrections visible.

Originality

We do not publish copied articles or mass-generated rewrites. Each report must add original structure, context, synthesis, verification, analysis or reporting beyond the material it relies on.

Sources and attribution

Claims that depend on external reporting, company announcements, research, public records or other sources are linked or clearly attributed. Primary sources are preferred whenever practical, and major claims should be cross-checked when independent confirmation is available.

Use of AI

AI tools may assist research, transcription, structure, translation, drafting and editing. AI is not treated as a source. Material factual claims must be checked against reliable sources before publication, and published copy is reviewed before it goes live.

Corrections

Material errors are corrected promptly. When a correction meaningfully changes a published report, the article should disclose the correction and its date. Readers can submit correction requests through our contact page.

Headlines

Headlines should accurately represent the underlying reporting. We avoid fabricated certainty, deceptive clickbait and headlines that materially overstate what the evidence supports.

Conflicts and sponsored content

Commercial relationships that could reasonably affect reader interpretation are disclosed. Sponsored or paid material must be clearly labeled and kept distinct from independent editorial reporting.

Updates

Fast-moving stories may be updated as new reliable information becomes available. Material changes should be reflected in the article's modification time and, when necessary for clarity, explained to readers.
