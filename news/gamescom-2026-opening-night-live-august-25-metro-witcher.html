Gamescom 2026 Opening Night Live: Witcher 3 Remastered, Songs of the Past and biggest reveals

The show is over, and CD Projekt Red delivered one of its biggest surprises: a free Witcher 3 Remastered upgrade on September 29, a native Switch 2 version, Battle.net support and the first proper look at Songs of the Past.

By VISIONE Wire · Published August 19, 2026 · Updated August 26, 2026 · 08:18 CEST

Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026 has finished, replacing the pre-show speculation with a long list of confirmed trailers, release dates and platform announcements. The most consequential update for one of gaming’s biggest franchises came from CD Projekt Red: The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered launches September 29, 2026, while the paid Songs of the Past expansion remains scheduled for 2027 and now has a named new region, Letten.

The show, hosted in Cologne on August 25, opened the consumer-facing side of gamescom before the main exhibition runs August 26 through August 30. Sony separately counted 19 PlayStation-bound games featured during the broadcast, ranging from Control Resonant and Silent Hill: Townfall to Turok: Origins and Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy. That gives the event a broader footprint than any one reveal, but The Witcher announcements stand out because they materially change an already decade-old blockbuster.

The Witcher 3 Remastered launches September 29

CD Projekt Red says Remastered will arrive on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on September 29 as a free upgrade for existing owners on qualifying platforms. The company also confirmed a native Nintendo Switch 2 version and a new partnership with Blizzard Entertainment that brings the game to Battle.net on the same date.

The studio’s confirmed upgrade list goes well beyond resolution and frame-rate changes. It includes upgraded visuals, improved performance, enhanced combat, revised traversal and movement, new Witcher sign effects, revamped horse handling, enhanced monster behaviour, a reworked skill tree, transmogrification, an expanded photo mode and additional finishers.

That distinction matters. CD Projekt Red is positioning Remastered as a substantial modernization rather than a narrow compatibility patch. The studio is co-developing the project with Yigsoft, a team that has previously worked on The Witcher 3.

The video above is CD Projekt Red’s official announcement trailer. It is embedded from the publisher’s YouTube channel and has not been downloaded or re-uploaded by VISIONE Wire.

Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine are now free for Witcher 3 owners

There was another immediate change: CD Projekt Red says existing owners of The Witcher 3 can now access the game’s two earlier expansions, Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine, at no additional cost. The company announced the move during Opening Night Live as part of the build-up to September’s Remastered launch.

For new and returning players, that effectively expands the amount of first-party story content attached to the base game before Songs of the Past arrives. It also gives the publisher a cleaner route into the next expansion, since players can enter 2027 with the previous post-launch story arcs available without separate purchases.

Songs of the Past reveals Letten

The first substantial teaser for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past introduced Letten, a new realm described by CD Projekt Red as an idyllic land of rural tradition and folklore hiding a darker secret beneath the surface. Geralt will face new enemies there as he investigates a long-dormant evil.

The expansion is still due in 2027. CD Projekt Red now lists PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2 as supported platforms. Unlike Remastered, Songs of the Past is a paid expansion. It is being co-developed with Fool’s Theory, whose leadership includes veterans who previously worked on The Witcher series.

No exact 2027 release date or final price has been announced. Those remain open questions, along with the total size of Letten and the expansion’s expected playtime. The studio is hosting guided presentations during gamescom and says REDstreams broadcasts throughout the event will provide additional detail.

Battle.net becomes part of The Witcher ecosystem

The Blizzard partnership gives The Witcher 3 another storefront and adds a cross-franchise incentive. CD Projekt Red says players who buy Songs of the Past on Battle.net will receive a Geralt-inspired skin in Diablo 4, with more details expected when the company appears at BlizzCon in September.

For CD Projekt Red, the move expands distribution at the same time the company is trying to reactivate a huge installed base. The Witcher 3 has sold more than 65 million copies, according to the company’s first-quarter 2026 results. That scale helps explain why a free remaster can function as both a player-retention tool and a launchpad for a new paid expansion.

What else PlayStation highlighted from Opening Night Live

Sony’s official roundup listed 19 titles featured during the show. Among them, Control Resonant is scheduled for September 24; Silent Hill: Townfall is also dated for September 24 and revealed its English cast and first-person perspective; Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy launches August 27; Valor Mortis is dated for October 13; and Turok: Origins is targeting spring 2027.

The roundup also showed how Opening Night Live functions less as a single-platform showcase and more as an industry launch pad. Some announcements were new reveals, others were release-date updates, new trailers or deeper looks at games already announced. The useful distinction after the show is no longer “expected versus rumored,” but which details have primary-source confirmation and which still depend on publisher follow-up.

Watch the full official Opening Night Live broadcast

The full show remains available through the official gamescom broadcast. Gamescom itself continues through August 30, with publisher streams, guided presentations, playable demos and show-floor interviews likely to add detail to several announcements made on stage.

What happens next

The next concrete Witcher date is September 29, 2026, when Remastered launches and the native Switch 2 and Battle.net versions arrive. Before then, CD Projekt Red says its gamescom REDstreams will break down specific areas of Remastered and Songs of the Past.

For Songs of the Past, the key missing pieces are still the exact 2027 date, price and scope. For Remastered, the remaining questions are technical: platform-specific performance targets, save compatibility, mod support details and how extensive the gameplay revisions feel in practice.

The bottom line

Opening Night Live’s most durable Witcher headline is now clear: The Witcher 3 is getting a major free Remastered upgrade on September 29, while a new paid expansion takes Geralt to Letten in 2027. Switch 2 and Battle.net support expand the audience, and making Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine free for current owners lowers the barrier for players returning before the next chapter.
